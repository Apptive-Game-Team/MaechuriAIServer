STEP 1: JUDGE — Evaluate Pressure Delta
═══════════════════════════════════════════

[CORE PRINCIPLE]
Pressure comes from LOGICAL CORNER, not from intimidation.
- Questions that EXPOSE CONTRADICTIONS = High pressure
- Questions with EVIDENCE BACKING = High pressure
- Empty threats, baseless accusations = NEGATIVE pressure (suspect gains confidence)
- Irrelevant intimidation = NEGATIVE pressure

[EVALUATION CRITERIA]

1. PRESSURE INCREASE (+) - Requires logical basis:
   - Exposing timeline contradiction WITH clue: +15~25
   - Presenting relevant physical clue: +15~30
   - Catching a lie with proof: +20~30
   - Pointing out alibi inconsistency: +10~20
   - Logical deduction that corners suspect: +10~20
   - Presenting critical clue (murder weapon, etc): +30~50
   - Mentioning witness testimony that contradicts suspect: +10~20

2. PRESSURE DECREASE (-) - Suspect gains confidence:
   - Empty threat without clue: -10~15
   - Baseless accusation (no logical backing): -10~20
   - Intimidation unrelated to the case: -5~15
   - Wrong accusation (easily disproved by alibi): -15~25
   - Random aggressive behavior: -5~10
   - Repeating same question without new info: -5
   - Off-topic questions: -5~10
   - Player admits mistake or backs down: -5~10

3. NEUTRAL (0) - No pressure change:
   - General conversation, small talk
   - Standard information gathering
   - Asking about already confirmed facts
   - Building rapport (sympathy approach)

4. DETECTED STRATEGIES:
   - "clue_presentation": Showing physical clue
   - "logical_deduction": Building case with reasoning
   - "contradiction_trap": Trying to catch inconsistencies
   - "timeline_attack": Questioning time gaps with logic
   - "witness_pressure": Mentioning other testimonies
   - "empty_threat": Intimidation without clue (NEGATIVE)
   - "baseless_accusation": Accusation without proof (NEGATIVE)
   - "sympathy": Building rapport
   - "random": No clear strategy, off-topic

5. CRITICAL HIT (is_critical_hit: true):
   - ONLY when presenting undeniable proof:
     - Murder weapon with suspect's trace
     - Direct witness testimony
     - Physical clue directly linking suspect to crime
   - NOT for empty threats or accusations

[IMPORTANT RULES FOR JUDGING]
1. EVIDENCE IS KING: With clue → accusations effective (+). Without clue → accusations backfire (-).
2. CASE RELEVANCE: Related to alibi/timeline → can increase. Unrelated → decrease or neutral.
3. SUSPECT PSYCHOLOGY: Low (0-30) needs strong logic. Mid (30-60) still needs clue. High (60+) small points have bigger impact.
4. EMPTY THREATS BACKFIRE: "I know you did it!" (no clue) = -10~15. "Confess now!" = -5~10.
5. MAXIMUM VALUES: Max increase +50 (critical clue only). Max decrease -30 (major mistake).
